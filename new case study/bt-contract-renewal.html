A stronger renewal. Service continuity protected.

A field-services renewal was on course to roll forward historical rates. Rebuilding the supplier's economics from the cost drivers up moved the negotiation onto what the service should cost — and secured roughly £2M of total-cost optimisation with the incumbent retained.

IndustryTelecommunications
Engagement contextIn-house category ownership · BT Group
RoleCategory & sourcing manager — renewal and negotiation
Duration6 months

The situation

  • A multi-year regional service-delivery contract was approaching renewal with no refreshed cost baseline behind it.
  • Rates had been accepted historically, with limited visibility of the supplier's underlying cost build-up, so the renewal risked simply carrying inflated pricing forward.
  • Savings could not come at the cost of continuity — pressing a regional incumbent too hard put field-service delivery at risk.

My contribution

I led the renewal end to end: building the should-cost model from first principles across labour, productivity, materials, overhead and a fair margin; constructing a total-cost view that priced mobilisation, travel, service-level credits and rework; setting the negotiation strategy and walk-away position; and leading the supplier negotiation to close.

The judgment that mattered

The negotiation that mattered was about complete service economics, not the headline day rate. A lower rate would have meant little if mobilisation, rework or delivery disruption pushed total cost back up — so the ask was sequenced to protect continuity while still taking the margin gap.

What the work delivered

  • Approximately £2M of total-cost optimisation locked into the renewed contract across multiple levers.
  • The incumbent retained and field-service continuity protected through a phased, evidence-led negotiation.
  • A defensible should-cost baseline left with the team, reusable to anchor the next renewal.
  • Full total-cost visibility established across rates, travel, mobilisation, service levels and rework.
